      To use your own playlist: on YouTube, open the playlist, tap Share → Copy link and paste it here. Public and unlisted playlists work. Open my YouTube playlists ↗ · Find ADHD focus music ↗

      The video plays in a small player (YouTube needs it to stay visible). On a phone, tap ▶ on the player once. Live streams like Lofi Girl never end, so they just keep playing.

      On phones, YouTube only plays while the hourglass is on screen. Playing with the screen off or in another app is a YouTube Premium feature. During a session the screen stays on, and the music picks up again when you come back. For music that keeps going while you use other apps, pick Spotify.

      Your playlist

        On your phone, Spotify plays in the Spotify app. Full songs on the free plan too, and the music keeps going when you switch apps or lock the screen. Tap a playlist to open it in Spotify, or just press Start: the session opens your selected one there.
        To add one: in Spotify, tap ⋯ → Share → Copy link and paste it here.

        1. Log in to Spotify ↗ in this browser, so full songs play from your account (Free or Premium). Without it, Spotify plays 30-second previews.
        2. In Spotify, open a playlist, tap ⋯ → Share → Copy link and paste it here. Open my Spotify playlists ↗

        When a track ends

        Want your YouTube or Spotify playlists with your sessions? They play in the full app: open it ↗ and install it from your browser.

        Browsers only allow sound after you tap or click the page, so tap once after opening it. On a phone speaker, Lo-fi and Rain come through best; the deeper sounds need headphones.

          Keep it on your desktop

          1. Chrome: open this page, then menu ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app (or Create shortcut, with “Open as window” ticked).
          2. Edge: menu ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
          3. Safari on Mac: File → Add to Dock.
          4. Floating timer: click Float mini timer (or press P) before you minimise. A small hourglass stays on top of every window; drag it anywhere and pull its corner to resize.
